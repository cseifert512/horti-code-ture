# Clematis viticella 'Étoile Violette'

**Common name:** Purple summer clematis 'Étoile Violette'  
**Accepted name:** RHS: *Clematis* 'Étoile Violette' (Vt) (record 92936); *Clematis viticella* 'Étoile Violette' is an RHS synonym.  
**Place in palette:** CORE — summer floral module

## Observational / horticultural baseline

| Trait | Currently supported description |
|---|---|
| Growth habit | Deciduous clematis; Group 3 pruning. |
| Climbing or support mechanism | Twining leaf petioles. |
| RHS ultimate-height envelope | RHS: 2.5–4 m; generally 2–5 years to ultimate size class. |
| Hardiness | H6 (RHS). |
| Flower / leaf / fruit season | Velvety deep-purple flowers, typically mid to late summer; winter stems leafless. |
| Aspect and exposure | Full sun/partial shade; adaptable aspects and exposed/sheltered locations per RHS. |
| Soil | Moist but well-drained; neutral/alkaline preferred. |

**Evidence discipline:** height classes and mature size times are qualitative UK horticultural descriptors, **not** yearly extension, support-path reach or physical force values. This file makes no claim to cultivar-calibrated numerical growth rates.

## Species-specific biological logic

Flowers on **current-season** stems. Hard pruning in late winter affects current growth architecture but does not remove the prior-year flowering cohort as in Group 1 clematis.

## What Bower must provide: local supports

Fine wires / thin lattice capable of being grasped by developing petioles; prune access right from its basal crown.

## Global structure placement logic

Lower 2.5–4 m, ideally visually prominent under the woody canopy and on separate narrow supports.

## Gardener intervention / pruning

Group 3 cut back in late winter/early spring then retrain and monitor for canopy entanglement; can be repeated annually.

## Interactions with the broader palette

Complements wisteria after its spring display, provided adequate summer light reaches the clematis.

## Risks, exclusions and scientific caveats

Do not assign wisteria twining/flower-spur rules; root competition and shading may strongly reduce flowers. Skin irritant; harmful to pets (rabbits) if eaten (RHS).

## Parameters to obtain or calibrate

Annual basal shoot generation after cutting; petiole grasp behavior; seasonal flower density and leaf area; re-growth rate by plant age.

At minimum, bind all measured parameters to plant **age**, plant **part**, time step, site/climate, support properties and propagation/stock history. Keep missing values empty rather than extrapolating from species names or RHS maturity classes.
