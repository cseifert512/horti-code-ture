# Rosa 'The Generous Gardener'

**Common name:** Climbing rose (Ausdrawn)  
**Place in palette:** CORE OPTION — low/middle fragrance and pink repeated bloom

## Observational / horticultural baseline

| Trait | Currently supported description |
|---|---|
| Growth habit | Woody climbing rose with arching, thorny canes; commonly loses most leaves over UK winter. |
| Climbing or support mechanism | Scrambling canes; requires human tie-in/training — not a botanical twiner. |
| RHS ultimate-height envelope | RHS descriptive height up to ~4.5 m (size category lists 4–8 m). |
| Hardiness | Confirm local RHS rating; cold survival is not the dominant geometric issue. |
| Flower / leaf / fruit season | Strongly scented semi-double pale-pink flowers, repeat-flowering from midsummer to autumn. |
| Aspect and exposure | Full sun/partial shade; flower best where sufficient light reaches the cane framework. |
| Soil | Moist but well-drained fertile soil. |

**Evidence discipline:** height classes and mature size times are qualitative UK horticultural descriptors, **not** yearly extension, support-path reach or physical force values. This file makes no claim to cultivar-calibrated numerical growth rates.

## Species-specific biological logic

Perennial canes with seasonally produced flowering laterals; different flowering and renewal dynamics from wisteria. Include cane age and repeated flushes as separate states.

## What Bower must provide: local supports

Strong and reachable tie points, training directions that can encourage lateral flowering shoots, and comfortable distance from people due to thorns.

## Global structure placement logic

3–5 m human-facing arch/entry sector rather than overhead 12m crown.

## Gardener intervention / pruning

Explicit gardener tie-in, pruning, renewal of old canes and possible deadheading; inspect thorn/public interface.

## Interactions with the broader palette

Adds summer pink/pale bloom complementing purple wisteria/clematis; give independent maintenance route.

## Risks, exclusions and scientific caveats

Thorns and falling flowers near public circulation; **no self-climb** without gardener-created ties.

## Parameters to obtain or calibrate

Cane age/replacement, response to lateral orientation, bloom flush probability, tied-canopy geometry, pruning effect and flower survival.

At minimum, bind all measured parameters to plant **age**, plant **part**, time step, site/climate, support properties and propagation/stock history. Keep missing values empty rather than extrapolating from species names or RHS maturity classes.
