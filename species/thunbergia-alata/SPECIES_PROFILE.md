# Thunbergia alata

**Common name:** Black-eyed Susan vine  
**Place in palette:** CORE ANNUAL OPTION — replaceable warm-season colour

## Observational / horticultural baseline

| Trait | Currently supported description |
|---|---|
| Growth habit | Tender evergreen perennial in frost-free climates, **grown as an annual outdoors in UK**. |
| Climbing or support mechanism | Herbaceous stem twining, right-handed helix: anticlockwise seen from above (Darwin). |
| RHS ultimate-height envelope | RHS: 1.5–2.5 m; 1–2 years to ultimate size category; seasonal performance depends on stock and warmth. |
| Hardiness | H2 (RHS), not winter-hardy outdoors in the UK. |
| Flower / leaf / fruit season | Bright orange/yellow/cream (cultivar-dependent) with dark centres; summer to early autumn. |
| Aspect and exposure | Sunny, warm, sheltered. |
| Soil | Free-draining soil with reliable moisture. |

**Evidence discipline:** height classes and mature size times are qualitative UK horticultural descriptors, **not** yearly extension, support-path reach or physical force values. This file makes no claim to cultivar-calibrated numerical growth rates.

## Species-specific biological logic

Short-season establishment, rapid herbaceous internode extension, late-season death/reset or removal outdoors.

## What Bower must provide: local supports

Light, **removable** fine trellis and accessible replacement root pocket; not part of the permanent upper woody scaffold.

## Global structure placement logic

Pedestrian-level colour pockets ~1.5–2.5m, especially during early permanent-climber establishment years.

## Gardener intervention / pruning

Gardener replants each late spring after frost risk, trains shoots, waters, removes annual vines when they decline; replacement annual event explicit.

## Interactions with the broader palette

Provides temporary vibrant colour, but should not choke long-lived young clematis and wisteria leaders.

## Risks, exclusions and scientific caveats

Not a solution for winter flowers or evergreen coverage in a UK freestanding Bower.

## Parameters to obtain or calibrate

Seasonal extension and internode lengths, flowers per node, photoperiod/temperature response, establishment and frost kill.

At minimum, bind all measured parameters to plant **age**, plant **part**, time step, site/climate, support properties and propagation/stock history. Keep missing values empty rather than extrapolating from species names or RHS maturity classes.
