# Sarcococca confusa

**Common name:** Sweet box  
**Place in palette:** GROUND COMPANION — winter fragrance, NOT a climber

## Observational / horticultural baseline

| Trait | Currently supported description |
|---|---|
| Growth habit | Evergreen low shrub; **not a climbing plant**. |
| Climbing or support mechanism | None; bushy shoot development only. |
| RHS ultimate-height envelope | RHS: ~1–1.5 m size category. |
| Hardiness | H5 (RHS). |
| Flower / leaf / fruit season | Small cream-white highly scented winter flowers, dark glossy leaves year round, occasional dark berries. |
| Aspect and exposure | Partial to full shade, sheltered; useful at shaded entrances. |
| Soil | Humus-rich moist but well-drained; some tolerance of dry shade once established. |

**Evidence discipline:** height classes and mature size times are qualitative UK horticultural descriptors, **not** yearly extension, support-path reach or physical force values. This file makes no claim to cultivar-calibrated numerical growth rates.

## Species-specific biological logic

Multi-stemmed shrub with bush canopy and ground root station; separate geometry generator from all climbing categories.

## What Bower must provide: local supports

Soil bed only, **no local climbing trellis**.

## Global structure placement logic

Adjacent shaded path, winter doorway or indoor-outdoor scent threshold; does not colonise upper Bower structure.

## Gardener intervention / pruning

Maintain access and form, mulch/irrigate on establishment; no vine training.

## Interactions with the broader palette

Winter human experience without competition for overhead support; limited root/soil competition with large climbers if placed nearby.

## Risks, exclusions and scientific caveats

Aesthetic effect is scent and glossy green rather than large winter flowers visible from across the site.

## Parameters to obtain or calibrate

Bush branching and height, winter flower density, scent qualitative output, shade tolerance, root station overlap.

At minimum, bind all measured parameters to plant **age**, plant **part**, time step, site/climate, support properties and propagation/stock history. Keep missing values empty rather than extrapolating from species names or RHS maturity classes.
