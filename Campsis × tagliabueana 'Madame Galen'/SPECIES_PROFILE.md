# Campsis × tagliabueana 'Madame Galen'

**Common name:** Trumpet creeper 'Madame Galen'  
**Place in palette:** OPTION — warm/sheltered late-summer display

## Observational / horticultural baseline

| Trait | Currently supported description |
|---|---|
| Growth habit | Large vigorous deciduous woody climber. |
| Climbing or support mechanism | Aerial/adventitious roots; needs suitable contact surface and may need support training. |
| RHS ultimate-height envelope | RHS: 8–12 m; 5–10 years to ultimate class. |
| Hardiness | H4 (RHS). |
| Flower / leaf / fruit season | Large vivid reddish-orange tubular flowers in late summer to autumn; winter leafless. |
| Aspect and exposure | Full sun, preferably south/west, warm **sheltered** position; heat strongly affects flowering reliability. |
| Soil | Moist but well-drained fertile site. |

**Evidence discipline:** height classes and mature size times are qualitative UK horticultural descriptors, **not** yearly extension, support-path reach or physical force values. This file makes no claim to cultivar-calibrated numerical growth rates.

## Species-specific biological logic

Builds long-lived woody framework with seasonal flowering laterals; do not automatically use old-wood wisteria bud-induction rules.

## What Bower must provide: local supports

Strong substantial framework, prepared root-adhesion surface or explicit tie points; large open routes, not presumed self-adherent on a thin smooth metal wire.

## Global structure placement logic

Sunny warm external face/edge, as a stronger late-summer flower alternative; poor match for cool exposed high canopy.

## Gardener intervention / pruning

Prune late winter to maintain size/flowering framework; monitor vigorous shoots and contact integrity.

## Interactions with the broader palette

Warm-season colour complement but can become a large competitor for wisteria and evergreens.

## Risks, exclusions and scientific caveats

H4 and heat preference reduce portability across UK climates; strong growth/woody mass, flower performance in cool summers uncertain.

## Parameters to obtain or calibrate

Bud and current-year lateral growth behaviour, heat/insolation flowering response, aerial-root contact substrate test, stem load, pruning.

At minimum, bind all measured parameters to plant **age**, plant **part**, time step, site/climate, support properties and propagation/stock history. Keep missing values empty rather than extrapolating from species names or RHS maturity classes.
